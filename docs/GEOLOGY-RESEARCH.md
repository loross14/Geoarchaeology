# Geological acquisition and chronology audit

Date: 2026-09-06. Geographic scope fixed before inspection: Michigan and Ohio. Geological records were sought by state, sediment-core terminology and chronology; no archaeological-site proximity filter was used. This is a bounded convenience pilot, not a representative sample or an estimate of statewide activity.

## Imported baseline

Seven inherited physical cores, 226 extracted intervals. Original raw Michigan-Ohio-Core-Data.json is untouched. The compact export removes duplicate raw catalogue/table payloads while retaining normalized intervals, recovered-length values, original flags, source row/page locators and suppressed duplicate row IDs. Michigan description fields are material/modifier combinations; Ohio descriptions are inherited extractions, not certified verbatim quotations. No new sediment ages were inferred.

County joins: Muskegon 26121; Allegan 26005; Kalamazoo 26077; Calhoun 26025; Union 39159; Sandusky 39143; Guernsey 39059. C03051 remains provisionally assigned to catalogue Sandusky County with an unresolved catalogue/log association. This join is inventory organization, not approval for precise spatial inference.

C03082 logged bottom remains 24.5ft; competing catalogue depths and tentative paleosol remain flagged. Core loss, removed specimens, disturbance and geological erosion are not interchangeable. All seven sediment ages and prior archaeological reuse remain unaudited/unknown as appropriate.

## Search route

Initial primary-archive queries: `site.ncei.noaa.gov Michigan lake sediment calibrated years BP core pollen`; equivalent Ohio query; then `"Silver Lake, Ohio" "2012" sediment`, `"Michigan" "lake-level" sediment core "cal" "Henne"`, and `"Ohio" "sediment core" "calibrated" lake chronology`. Search results for Ohio River lakes in Indiana/Kentucky were excluded geographically. Michigan metal-sediment NOAA results were screened but not admitted; no usable chronology was extracted. These are search-screening exclusions, not findings about preservation.

Three detailed candidates were inspected:

1. **Silver Lake, Summit County — Lally (2016).** [Primary thesis](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=akron1479127321585964&disposition=attachment). The 13.5m composite has local radiocarbon control, cross-lake correlations and an assumed deglacial basal constraint. Section 3.9 uses OxCal 4.2 and substantial old-carbon offsets. Printed p.74 calls 1875 CE 141 cal BP, whereas 1950-based conversion is 75 BP; recent chronology therefore needs reference-year reconciliation. Table 15 and Figure 39 require review before admission. This issue does not invalidate every older horizon, but blocks automatic conversion of the published curve into a uniform cal-BP dataset. Next: verify individual laboratory determinations, material and corrections, calibration uncertainty, correlation controls and time origin. A related Swisher/Peck 2020 primary article was found, but both journal/OSU routes returned access-denied pages; no workaround used.

2. **Nelson Lake, eastern Upper Peninsula — Delcourt et al. (2002).** [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1006/qres.2001.2308). Abstract supports local sediment-proxy changes at 5300 and 3000 cal yr BP, including inferred paludification. Only abstract/reference content was accessible. Next: obtain full methods and age table, identify core coordinates/county and establish uncertainty. The abstract alone cannot establish the spatial support or model required by this release.

3. **Ohio/eastern Indiana lake-and-bog network — Glover et al. (2011).** [Institutional record](https://openworks.wooster.edu/facpub/90/). Authors independently collected 29 lake/bog records and report basal radiocarbon evidence for early retreat. Primary publisher and institutional abstracts were inspected. Per-core locations, date calibration, sample material and uncertainty were not accessible in those records. Next: retrieve full article/data or associated Cincinnati thesis, then admit only individually verified Ohio records. Do not turn the regional 22 ka abstract statement into county-wide deglaciation dates.

## Admission result

**landscape.json is intentionally empty.** No inspected additional record passed both chronology and spatial-support gates within this bounded pass. Candidate records are preserved in candidates.json and the source register, outside the temporal evidence layer. The working site can filter sourced archaeological periods, but cannot yet claim a reconstructed geological landscape animation. Unknown geology must stay unknown at every date.

## Novelty and next comparison

Geological provenance is established for the acquisition route; archaeological non-use is not. A later bounded reuse audit should search each core identifier, report DOI, lake name and lab sample IDs across archaeological publications and cite both queries and hits. No claim of never evaluated, novel discovery, intact buried occupation surface or greater survival depth than excavation has been demonstrated here. Investigation footprint, maximum tested depth and common elevation datum remain necessary for any depth-gap comparison.
